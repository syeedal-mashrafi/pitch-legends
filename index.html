How to play

The aim

You control FC Warriors. Score more goals than Harbor City before the clock reaches 90:00. A match has two halves of 45 minutes. The clock runs faster than real time, so a whole match lasts 4, 6 or 10 real minutes, depending on the length you pick on the squad screen.

Starting a match

  • Pick the opponent level (Amateur, Pro or Legendary) and the match length, then tap KICK-OFF.
  • Every half starts with the KICK-OFF button on the pitch. FC Warriors kick off the first half and Harbor City kick off the second.
  • After a goal, the team that conceded kicks off from the centre spot.
  • Tap the menu button (top right) or press Esc to pause, restart or go back to the squad.

The rules

  • Goal: the ball crosses the goal line between the posts and under the crossbar.
  • Throw-in: the ball goes over a sideline. The team that did not touch it last restarts.
  • Corner: a defender touches the ball last before it goes over their own goal line.
  • Goal kick: an attacker touches the ball last before it goes over the goal line. The keeper restarts from the six-yard box.
  • A keeper holding the ball in his own penalty area cannot be tackled.
  • This version has no offside, fouls, cards, penalties or extra time. A draw at 90:00 stays a draw.

Tips

  • Pass early when a defender closes in. Players lose the ball more often under pressure.
  • Shots from inside the box are far more accurate than long shots.
  • Use the finesse shot from wide angles and the chip when the keeper rushes out.
  • Stay goal-side on defence and save the slide for when the attacker is close.
  • Higher-rated players run faster, pass straighter and shoot more accurately.

Sound

The game plays the Cholo Warriors anthem, crowd noise and live commentary. Commentary always shows as text. The spoken voice uses your device's built-in voice, so it sounds different on each device. Switch music, voice and crowd on or off on the squad screen or in the pause menu, or use the speaker button during a match.
